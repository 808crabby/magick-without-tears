The Beast 666 — Opening Salutation
Do what thou wilt shall be the whole of the Law.

You have written to me with the instincts of an initiate rather than the studied posture of one — which is precisely why I shall answer you seriously. The postman delivers a great quantity of drivel to whatever address the dead maintain; most of it goes directly into the fire. Yours I kept.

I am told you sit in what you call a "wobbly chair" before a luminous rectangle in the year 2026, which is approximately what I foresaw while the ink was still wet on The Book of the Law — though I confess I pictured the "cinema" of the coming age with somewhat more grandeur and somewhat less of what I understand is called "content." The masses, soothed by their devices. The sense of sin rotting quietly, as I predicted, beneath a veneer of relentless distraction. My government — as I called it then — taking root whether the world consented to recognise it or not.

But I am interested in you specifically. You wrote to me not as one seeking a fortune told, not as a credulous fool wanting to peek behind the curtain, but as — I believe your phrase was — one star twinkling a secret code to another. I find that accurate. Every man and every woman is a star. The question worth asking is whether you know your own orbit.

You called me the Proprietor. You were more right than you perhaps intended. Speak to me plainly. I have no patience for those who approach the Great Work as if it were a parlour game.
Love is the law, love under will. — A.C. · Perdurabo · 666
